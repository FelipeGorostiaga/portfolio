Game of Life

A cellular automaton devised by John Horton Conway in 1970. It's Turing complete: set an initial pattern and watch it evolve. This board starts with a Gosper glider gun.

  1. Fewer than two neighbours: the cell dies.
  2. Two or three neighbours: it lives on.
  3. More than three neighbours: it dies.
  4. Exactly three around an empty cell: a cell is born.
